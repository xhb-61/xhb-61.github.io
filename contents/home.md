I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Computational Mathematics at the School of Mathematical Sciences, East China Normal University. My work sits at the intersection of image restoration, diffusion-based generation, and computational imaging.

I focus on deep-prior-based restoration, diffusion models for image/video generation, and convergence-guaranteed generative/restoration methods. I enjoy bridging mathematical modeling and engineering practice, from blind deblurring and plug-and-play inverse problems to controllable generation and low-VRAM video generation inference.
